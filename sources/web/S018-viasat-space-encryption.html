Providing complete space-ground encryption

Space encryption is about more than just protecting satellites, subsystems, and mission data — it’s about securing the entire space-ground system. Viasat takes a holistic approach, working closely with customers to develop innovative yet simplified security solutions that support low Earth orbit (LEO), medium Earth orbit (MEO), and geostationary Earth orbit (GEO) satellite missions in new ways.
